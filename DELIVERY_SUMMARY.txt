DATA MODELS (Complete Serialization Support)
================================================================================

ENUMS (with @SerializedName):
  - CabinClass: ECONOMY, PREMIUM_ECONOMY, BUSINESS, FIRST
  - TripType: ROUNDTRIP, ONE_WAY
  - PriceLabel: STEAL, GREAT_DEAL, FAIR, UNKNOWN
  - PriceTrend: UP, DOWN, STABLE
  - ActionType: BUY_NOW, WAIT, SET_ALERT

REQUEST/RESPONSE:
  - IntentRequest: {query: String}
  - IntentResponse: {parsedRequest: SearchRequest, interpretation: String, confidence: Float}
  - SearchRequest: origin, destination, departure_date, return_date, adults, children, infants,
                   cabin_class, direct_only, trip_type
  - SearchResponse: search_id, origin, destination, departure_date, return_date, offers[], 
                    search_completed_at, currency

FLIGHT DETAILS:
  - FlightOffer: id, outboundItinerary, returnItinerary, price, baggage_info, fare_conditions,
                 price_intelligence, seats_remaining, fare_class
  - Segment: departure_airport, arrival_airport, departure_time, arrival_time, airline,
             flight_number, aircraft, duration_minutes, stops
  - Itinerary: segments[], total_duration_minutes
  - PriceBreakdown: base_fare, taxes, fees, total
  - BaggageInfo: carry_on, checked_bags
  - FareConditions: refundable, changeable, seat_selection_included
  - PriceIntelligence: percentile, trend, predicted_7d, predicted_14d, price_label, 
                       action_type, action_reason
